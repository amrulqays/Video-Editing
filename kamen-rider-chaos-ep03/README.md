# Kamen Rider Chaos, Episode 3: 「闇ノ刃、翠ノ拳」

![poster](poster.jpg)

This is a cinematic one-sheet for episode 3. It shows Umbrazor fighting the jade-eyed rider, staged in the style of the *Amazons THE MOVIE* teaser:

- The two riders meet on a single diagonal, with a white-hot flash at the point of contact.
- Thrown sumi ink fills the corners.
- Vertical kanji slogans sit in the top corners.
- The title block is in the lower centre, the episode mark is in the lower right where a release date would go, and the tagline runs along the bottom.
